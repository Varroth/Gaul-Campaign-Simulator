CURRENT MUSTERS

Order of battle

A unit in this builder is one abstracted tabletop squad or character. Datasheet names, basic characteristics and listed base points are linked to New Recruit when available. Infernal Warriors can choose published formation sizes, supported individual weapon menus and eligible attached Leaders. Advanced rules remain simplified. A point estimate is clearly marked when the source lacks a directly readable default cost. Expand a unit’s Verification details to see which fields are approximations. A synced profile is NOT automatically verified.

FIELD MANUAL

How to play

This alpha is a streamlined tactical board game inspired by tabletop wargaming, not an exact Warhammer 40,000 rules engine.

01

Build your forces

Open Army Builder, add squads and characters on both sides, then start a new battle. The starter armies are approximately 1,000 simulator points each.

02

Movement

Select a friendly unit, then click an unoccupied reachable tile. Walls and other units block paths. Each squad moves once per turn.

03

Shooting and charging

Click Next phase to enter shooting; select your unit then an enemy in range. In charge phase, select an attacker and an enemy to attempt a 2D6 charge.

04

Fight and score

During fight phase, attack adjacent enemies. After each round-two-or-later turn, score 5 VP per controlled objective. Model count × OC determines control.

05

End the round

After fight phase, advance to let the opponent play automatically. The battle ends after five rounds or when a force is destroyed.

06

Save and test

Save/load in this browser, export/import JSON, and run ten-game automated tests. Seeds reproduce the same outcomes with the same armies and engine version.

Alpha rules and known limitations

The simulator reads unit entries from the public New Recruit repository on page load and on request. The Silver Tempests New Recruit catalogue is currently empty, so its simulator units remain provisional. Only explicitly reviewed and revision-pinned combat mappings may earn the Verified badge; none are automatically granted. Infernal Warriors configuration supports source-linked formations, flat per-model weapon menus, simplified single-Leader attachments, Reserve plans, and eligible ground-transport manifests. Published per-datasheet roster limits, named Leader uniqueness, shared Lost Cohort cap, five source-audited weapon quotas, purchased Reserves, Bloodletter Invocation and five ground-transport capacities are modeled. Other squad-specific constraints, special rules and detachments may not be represented. Some unit points are placeholders marked as estimates or calculations. Combat uses D6 hit, wound, armour save, individual damage allocation and optional Feel No Pain. Terrain cover modifies saving throws; wall-only line of sight, stratagems, command points, full psychic tests, full transport and mission-specific Reserves rules, model-by-model movement, fully accurate Leader attachment rules, and faction detachment rules are not yet implemented.

Infernal Warriors receive simplified 5+ Feel No Pain, one wound of start-of-turn recovery, and an ordinary Warp-Aggravated Pain test near hostile Psykers or Daemons. Supported weapon traits include Torrent, Rapid Fire, Heavy, Twin-linked, Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Melta, Blast, Pistol, Ignores Cover and Devastating Wounds. Abandoned Dead Ghost-Shroud increases required ranged Hit rolls at long range. A nearby Abandoned Dead Nullifier makes simulated psychic shooting less accurate. Silver Tempests psychic mastery is represented by ranged attacks; Frozen Cogs containment has not been modeled. These simplifications are explicitly not final faction rules.
